The Heritage of ArgyleKnitUp

Preserving traditional Scottish intarsia diamond knitting, hand-linked seamless toe craftsmanship, and fine Saxon merino hosiery in the historic cast-iron district of Manhattan.

Our Hosiery Philosophy

In an era dominated by synthetic fast-fashion socks that stretch, slip, and wear into holes within months, ArgyleKnitUp champions the patient art of generational hosiery knitting. Established in 1994 along Mercer Street in New York, our atelier operates on a foundational premise of extreme material purity, seamless anatomical comfort, and decadal durability.

We believe luxury socks are not an afterthought, but the bedrock substrate of sartorial poise. By knitting fine 17.5-micron Saxon merino wool on 200-needle frames and hand-linking every toe loop individually, we craft hosiery that looks impeccable beneath tailored suiting while providing all-day foot freshness.

Traditional Craft

Hand-Linked Toes

Every toe seam is manually impaled onto linking dial points and sewn stitch-by-stitch, eliminating bulky machine ridges completely.

Knitting Physics

200-Needle Frames

High-gauge cylinder knitting creates dense, fine-grained textile fabric with smooth stitch definition and vibrant diamond contrast.

Noble Fibers

Saxon Merino Fleece

17.5-micron long-staple fibers combed to eliminate short ends, yielding silky softness that thermoregulates feet in hot or cold weather.

The Mercer Street Atelier

Located at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, our Manhattan gallery and knitting workshop occupies a restored nineteenth-century brick loft. Visitors can witness wool yarns being wound onto cones, observe the rhythmic clicking of our intarsia circular frames, and feel the incredible softness of washed cashmere hosiery.
